What We Do

Services

  1. 01

    Recording

    Track vocals, live instruments or full bands in our acoustically treated live room. We handle microphone placement, gain staging and session management so you can focus entirely on your performance.

  2. 02

    Mixing

    Every element of your track balanced, shaped and placed in a three-dimensional soundstage. We work across genres — from Tamil film scores to independent hip-hop — with meticulous attention to clarity and feel.

  3. 03

    Mastering

    Loudness, dynamic range and tonal balance optimised for streaming platforms, vinyl or broadcast. Your final master delivered in all required formats.

  4. 04

    Vocal Production

    From melody writing and arrangement guidance to comping, tuning and processing — we help artists find and refine their vocal identity on record.
